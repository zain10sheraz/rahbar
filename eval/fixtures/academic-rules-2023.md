# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2023 edition)

This edition was replaced by the 2026 edition. It is kept for students admitted before 2026.

## 1. Attendance

1.1 A student must attend at least 75 percent of the lectures of each course to be allowed to sit the final examination.

1.2 A student who falls below the minimum attendance receives the grade FA in that course.

## 2. Academic warning

2.1 A student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 1.75 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic warning.

2.2 A student who remains on academic warning for two consecutive semesters is dropped from the programme.

## 3. Repeating a course

3.1 A student may repeat a course in which the grade was C or lower. When a course is repeated, the highest grade a student can receive is B.
